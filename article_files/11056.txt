Title: Reckless e-biker cuts into bus lane, nearly gets hit by bus and starts scolding bus driver

An electric biker was filmed arguing with a bus driver after he cut into the bus lane and nearly got hit by the bus. In a video posted by  All Singapore Stuff , the biker can be seen gesticulating wildly and even hitting the bus. According to the post, the biker "blamed the bus driver for not looking out for him". Facebook users slammed the biker for being careless and sympathised with the bus driver. Many called for more regulation for e-bikes and one user said: "We have motor, car, lorry, trailer licenses but none for bicycles and e-bikes."
