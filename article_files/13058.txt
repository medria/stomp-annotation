Title: What happened? Stationary car seen facing against traffic on CTE

A stationary car was seen facing against traffic on CTE on Monday (Jan 2). A clip of the facing the wrong way was uploaded on  Roads.sg . The clip shows a driver travelling on the right-most lane of the expressway. The driver slows down after spotting a car next to the divider, with the driver standing behind the vehicle. Some netizens warned the driver to switch his car's hazard lights on and not wait near the expressway lanes. Others, however, questioned why the driver whose car-cam filmed the incident was driving on the right-most lane when he was not overtaking any vehicles.
