Title: 

A man was seen allegedly taking multiple photos of a young woman without her knowledge on board bus service 195. Stomper Anonymous, who witnessed the incident, told Stomp it happened on Oct 9 at about 7am while the bus was heading towards Queenstown MRT station. According to the Stomper, the man was seated directly opposite the woman and was “repeatedly and overtly taking photographs of her” using his mobile phone. “He was holding the phone across the aisle and clearly aiming it at the lady’s face and upper body,” said the Stomper. “It was highly inappropriate and intrusive behaviour, and he did it multiple times.” The Stomper described the man as an “uncle type” wearing a light blue button-up shirt and having some white hair. He reportedly alighted at the bus stop outside Alexandra Hospital. The woman, who was wearing a light brown shirt, light blue jeans and a mask, appeared unaware of the man’s actions. “I was concerned for the lady’s privacy and safety,” said the Stomper. “This kind of behaviour needs to be reported to raise awareness."
