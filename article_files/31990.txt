Title: Police looking for woman involved in shop theft at Bugis Junction

The police are looking for a woman to assist with investigations into a case of shop theft which took place at Bugis Junction at 200 Victoria Street on Jan 3. Anyone with information is requested to call the police hotline at 1800-255-0000 or submit information online at  www.police.gov.sg/iwitness . All information will be kept strictly confidential.
