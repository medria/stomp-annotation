Title: Unleashed dog has near-accident with lorry in heart-stopping video

A small white dog caused a rather big commotion at a Choa Chu Kang traffic junction on Tuesday night (Jan 19). A Stomper alerted Stomp to a video of the incident that has been circulating on social media. According to the timestamp on the video, it happened at about 10.20pm.
