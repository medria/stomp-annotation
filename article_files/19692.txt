Title: Police appealing for next-of-kin of 58-year-old Ng Soon Lin to come forward

Mr Ng Soon Lin, Phillp, 58, has passed away, said the Police in an official statement. The deceased was a former resident of Blk 170 Ang Mo Kio Avenue 4, and the Police are appealing for the deceased’s next-of-kin to come forward. Anyone with information is requested to call the Police hotline at 1800-255-0000 or submit information online at  www.police.gov.sg/iwitness .  All information will be kept strictly confidential.
