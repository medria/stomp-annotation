Title: Van driver almost hits motorcycle while recklessly changing lanes along Bedok South Ave 1

Stomper  AL  sent Stomp a video showing a van driver nearly hitting a motorcyclist while recklessly changing lanes along Bedok South Avenue 1. According to the time stamp on the footage captured by a dashboard camera, the incident took place on Nov 4 at around 8.20am. In the video, the driver of a white van can be seen trying to filter into the right-most lane of a three-lane road without realising there was a motorcycle there. The van driver nearly hit the motorcyclist as he or she was switching lanes.  The motorcyclist stuck out his or her left leg, possibly to warn the van driver about his or her presence, before riding away.
