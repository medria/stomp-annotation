Title: Police looking for man involved in theft at Upper Thomson Road petrol station

The police are looking for a man to assist with investigations into a case of theft that occurred at a petrol station along Upper Thomson Road on May 11. Anyone with information is requested to call the police hotline at 1800-255- 0000 or submit information online via  www.police.gov.sg/iwitness . All information will be kept strictly confidential.
