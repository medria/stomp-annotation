Title: SMRT train captain brightens commuters' day with special National Day message

Commuters were pleasantly surprised to hear a special greeting from their train captain on  National Day  (Aug 9). Stomper  Joyce  told Stomp that she was travelling on the North-South Line from Orchard to Somerset at about 10.30am when she heard the announcement. She said her morning was made pleasant because of it. "This is what the captain said: 'This is your train captain Tan Kian Kuan speaking. On Singapore’s 58th birthday, I'd like to wish everyone, a happy National Day!' "Yeah, it felt like I am on a plane with a pilot. "Some of the commuters also reacted by looking around wondering what happened. "Happy Birthday Singapore!"
