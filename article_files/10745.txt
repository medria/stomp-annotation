Title: Have you seen my black wallet? I last saw it at Rivervale and contains around $800

A  Stomper  is appealing for help to locate his wallet that he last saw inside a car near around Block 187B Rivervale Drive on Sunday (Oct 30) at 6am after paying for a Grab ride. In a WhatsApp message, the Stomper said he was feeling tipsy from a night out and dozed off on a concrete chair after alighting the car. When he woke up, he realised that his wallet was not with him. He told Stomp in a WhatsApp message: "I have no idea if I left it behind in the Grab vehicle of if it was taken from me after I dozed off. "I remember paying for my Grab ride in cash." The Stomper's wallet contained a wad of $50 notes amounting to around $700 to $800. It is black in colour and has a money clip instead of a slot for notes. The wallet contains four credit cards as well as a replacement slip for a lost IC. It was purchased from The Wallet Shop but has no particular brand. Kindly contact us at  stomp@stomp.com.sg  or via WhatsApp at 9384 3761 if you have any information about the lost wallet.
