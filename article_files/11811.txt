Title: Daydreaming ah? Lorry mounts kerb while driving halfway along Punggol Walk

Stomper  Jimmy  spotted this errant lorry driver on Nov 19 at around 2pm at Punggol Walk, just outside Punggol Green Primary School. In a video from the Stomper, the lorry driver can be seen careening off to the side and mounting the kerb. Jimmy said, "The driver was being very dangerous and reckless; he was right outside the school," and wondered if the driver was daydreaming.
