Title: Old versus new: Which uniform for Singapore Police Force do you prefer?

All Singapore Police Force (SPF) ground officers will wear uniforms made of a new fabric designed to better deal with the heat and humidity, starting from next Monday (April 16). Made of 98 per cent polyester and 2 per cent spandex, it dries faster and has better sweat absorption capabilities, reported  The Straits Times . The new uniform - to be worn by police national servicemen and voluntary special constabularies as well - includes hidden plastic buttons in place of the metallic buttons on the current uniforms.
