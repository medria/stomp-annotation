Title: 2 men go at each other in Little India as others hold them back: 1 arrested for criminal intimidation

Tempers flared. Two men were caught on video "going at each other" at Mackenzie Road on Sunday afternoon (Oct 8), resulting in one man getting arrested. Stomper SS shared a video of what he called a "brouhaha at Little India". About a dozen people can be seen in the video as the fracas broke out in the open carpark. "A couple of guys were shouting at each other and needed to be held back," said the Stomper. "I'm not sure what transpired beforehand. "Afterwards, police were looking for the person in a pink shirt and his friends." In response to a Stomp query, the police said they were alerted to the dispute at 88 Mackenzie Road at 2.50pm. A 33-year-old man was arrested for criminal intimidation, said the police, adding that investigations are ongoing.
