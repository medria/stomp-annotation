Title: Billionaire Peter Lim's daughter reveals wedding date -- but we don't know who's the groom

Kim Lim, daughter of local billionaire Peter Lim, announced her wedding date via Instagram on Jul 21, three days after giving birth to a baby boy. The announcement came in the form of a  video post . In the video, the words "Save the date, 23rd March 2018," were written through animation on a wedding invitation. A caption on the video read:  "Stay tuned ❤ #blessed." The socialite however, stopped short of revealing the identity of her would-be husband. According to  AsiaOne , it was rumoured that the 25-year-old is seeing Mario Ho, who is the son of Hong Kong gambling magnate Stanley Ho. The two were spotted spending Valentine's Day together last year, and Ho was tagged in Lim's photo on Feb 15, 2016. We will find out who the lucky man is come March next year. Till then, we wish Kim all the best with her wedding preparations.
