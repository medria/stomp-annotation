Title: Spot the BKE lovebirds

Love can sprout in even the harshest of places, say, on an expressway.  Two motorcyclists occupied the middle lane on the BKE towards Woodlands on Tuesday (May 9) at around 12.30pm, with one rider even placing his foot on the other’s vehicle while the two chatted -- despite the oncoming traffic.  The pair of ‘lovebirds’ was captured on a dashboard camera on Stomper  Jeffery’ s car, and he subsequently alerted Stomp to the incident.  Said Jeffery: “I was shocked to see the rider placing his leg on the other bike and the two were talking.  “With so many heavy vehicles on the road, It can be dangerous. “I have no idea what these guys were thinking?”
