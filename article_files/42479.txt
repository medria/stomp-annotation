Article ID: 42479
Title: 

A driver challenged another motorist to a 'one-on-one fight' after he was honked at for trying to cut into his lane. A Stomper shared with Stomp footage of the incident and said it happened along Toh Guan Road on Nov 2 at about 7.20pm. The Stomper was driving and had to suddenly jam on his brakes when a Mazda attempted to cut in front of him. The camcar footage captured the Stomper honking at the other driver and shouting: "What you trying to do?" He then admonished him for trying to cut in front of him like that even though he had his signals on and called him a 'bloody idiot' before driving off. After a while, the Mazda driver caught up with the camcar driver at a traffic light. Seeing that the other driver did not want to drop the matter, he said: "You win, you win, I let you win, don't argue." The Mazda driver then cut in front of the Stomper's car to block him and gestured at him to pull over. When he stopped behind the Mazda, the driver got out of the car and told him to come out to fight 'one-on-one' but the Stomper refused. He then continued to taunt him and said: "I long time never punch people's face, come come come." The Stomper said that the other man's wife then persuaded him to return to the car and they drove off. "I feel disgusted and angry," said the Stomper. "He almost caused an accident yet didn't apologise and insisted that he signalled. "He also threatened to punch (my) face. "I'm traumatised and have arranged to see my psychologist next week." He has since made a police report and said that he intends to claim for mental health damage caused by this incident. The police confirmed with Stomp that a report was lodged.
