Title: Can you spot the creature camouflaged on tree bark at Buangkok?

Many creatures have adapted the ability to camouflage themselves for protection or to be able to spring upon their prey without warning. Stomper  Lau Lup  spotted one such creature while waiting at a bus stop in Buangkok yesterday (Apr 25). The creature in question was a long-legged spider blending in against the bark of a tree near the bus stop. Test your eyesight and try to spot it in the photo above.
