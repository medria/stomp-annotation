Title: Range Rover driver, why park on kerb when there are so many other lots at Parkway Parade?

A white range rover was seen parked on the kerb of the multi-storey carpark at Parkway Parade on Oct 27 at 2.45pm. Stomper  Ben  captured a video of the car being parked there with his in-car camera. In the video, the car can be seen on the kerb as Ben turns right further into the carpark. (Story continues below) Ben was shocked when he saw the car parked there. He said: "When I entered the carpark, the panel which specifies how many lots were free stated 122 lots. There were definitely other lots the car could have parked in. Why park there?"
