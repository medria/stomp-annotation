Title: HK actress Snow Suen looks like a goddess in pregnancy photoshoot

Hong Kong actress Snow Suen called it the best Valentine's Day present ever when she announced that she was three months pregnant on Feb 14. The 34-year-old star wedded her boyfriend of four years, Edwin Lo, on Dec 23 last year, just shortly before Christmas. Earlier in May this year, Snow posed for a photoshoot to celebrate close to seven months of pregnancy. Dreamy photos shared by  Oriental Sunday  show the actress looking like a goddess in the floral-themed shoot, and have earned Snow the admiration and well-wishes of netizens.
