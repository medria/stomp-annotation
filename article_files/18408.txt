Title: How Singapore used to look like in the 1971 according to an old tourist video

A video has emerged online showing how parts of Singapore look like in 1971. The video posted on  The Merlion Press  gives viewers a glimpse of how places of interest like Boat Quay and religious buildings used to look like. A part of the clip also shows an old-school hawker plying his trade the traditional way. Have a look at the video below and see Singapore in a different light.
