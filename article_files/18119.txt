Title: Front of tipper truck completely crushed after accident with 14 footer and lorry along AYE

Stomper  Saravanan  was driving along the Ayer Rajah Expressway (AYE) at around 4.20pm today (Jun 2) when he saw three vehicles involved in an accident after Pioneer Road North Roundabout. "There was one tipper truck, one 14 footer and one lorry," said Saravanan. "It looked to be very serious, I think whoever was inside must be severely injured." The front of the tipper truck was completely crushed and the lorry behind it looked to be badly damaged too. Other photos and a video circulating online show Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) personnel at the scene as well. Stomp has contacted the authorities for more information.
