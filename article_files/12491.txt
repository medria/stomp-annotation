Title: These photos show the rarely-seen side of Obama -- and will make you love him even more

Barack Obama is one of the most well-loved presidents in American history and has fans from around the globe. During his eight-year reign, official White House Photographer Pete Souza has taken about two million photos of Obama. Many of  these pictures  show a rare and different side to the politician that most people do not get to see. Check out all the images in the gallery above.
