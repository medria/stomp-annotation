Title: E-bikers stopped by enforcement officer at Commonwealth Ave West

Stomper  Vincent  was driving along Commonwealth Avenue West on Tuesday (May 15) at about 1.34pm when he saw a couple of e-bike riders being talked to by an enforcement officer. It is unclear why they had been pulled over. (Story continues after video) Some e-bikers have been featured in recent reports riding recklessly and endangering the lives of other motorists as well as their own while riding on the roads. Stomp reported on Tuesday that a  group of e-bikers were seen riding without helmets at Boon Lay Way . One of the girls riding pillion on one of the bikes was even sitting backwards sparking concerns for her safety.
