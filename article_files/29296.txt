Title: Discarded items at Ang Mo Kio void deck and staircase landing catch fire

Discarded items at the void deck of Block 473 Ang Mo Kio Avenue 10 and its seventh-floor staircase landing caught fire in the wee hours of Sunday (June 30). The Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) told Stomp that it responded to a call at 3.45am. A spokesman said: "Discarded items on fire were found at the void deck and Level 7 staircase landing. The fire was put out by members of the public prior to SCDF's arrival." Stomper  BC Chua , a resident at the block, saw charred remains at the staircase landing on Sunday, at around 8am. He added: "There was a charred item resembling a personal mobilty device (PMD). "The fire had also damaged the ceiling light and parts of the wall were black from the fire. "The following day (July 1), I saw police officers going around to ask residents if they had seen someone from CCTV footage, which I believe might be connected to the fire." The cause of the fire is under investigation.
