Title: 4 uncles arrested in enforcement operation against illegal gambling activities

Four men were arrested during an enforcement operation against gambling activities along Changi Road on Tuesday (Apr 7). The men, aged between 52 and 72, were nabbed for offences under the Common Gaming House Act, said police in a statement on Thursday. Cash amounting to $843 and gambling paraphernalia were seized during the operation conducted by Bedok Police Division. Investigations are ongoing. "The police will continue to take tough enforcement action against criminal activities," they added in the statement. "Those found engaging in illicit activities will be dealt with sternly in accordance with the law."
