Title: Volkswagen driver overtakes and hits Merc that cut in front of him

A Volkswagen and a Mercedes-Benz collided along an expressway after they took turns cutting in front of one another. A video of the incident was posted on SG Road Vigilante's Facebook group page showing what happened. It is unclear where or when the incident took place. In the  video , the Mercedes-Benz with licence plate number SLM9923S is seen cutting in front of the Volkwagen Jetta with licence plate number SKC7883L and signalling late. https://www.facebook.com/SGRVigilante/videos/558253291379460/ The Volkswagen is then seen suddenly overtaking the Mercedes, causing the Merc to hit the car's rear. Netizens have slammed both drivers for their 'immature' driving.
