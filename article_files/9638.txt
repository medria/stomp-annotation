Title: 35-year-old driver arrested for drink-driving after falling asleep at the wheel

A 35-year-old man was arrested in a case of drink driving at Holland Road after he fell asleep at the wheel earlier this morning(Sept 22). Stomper  Stan  reported that a car remained motionless for more than five minutes after the lights turned green. Other cars honked to wake up the driver and staff from a nearby Esso petrol station also went to check on him. Read also:  16 arrested in island-wide TP enforcement operations against drink-driving He also said the police arrived and interrogated the man for an hour before taking him away. The car was later towed away. A police spokesperson said: "On Sep 22, 4.57am, the police were on patrol along Holland Road when they conducted a check on a 35-year-old man" "He was subsequently arrested for suspected drink driving." "Police investigations are ongoing."
