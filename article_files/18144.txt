Title: Cyclist's foldable bicycles get stolen twice in 2 weeks near Boon Keng MRT Station

Stomper  Lim  wants to warn other cyclists who park their bicycles at Boon Keng Station to be wary of a thief he believes stole two of his foldable bicycles in the span of two weeks. "I lost two foldable bicycles in the span of two weeks and both incidents happened on a Monday - May 15 and May 29," said Lim. "I parked my bicycles at the bicycle parking bay below Block 104 Towner Road, nearest to the entrance of the MRT Station before catching a train to work. "Upon my return in the early evening they were gone." He also appealed to any witnesses who might have seen a "suspicious person" picking locks and riding off between 8am and 6pm on the above dates to inform the police.
