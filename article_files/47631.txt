Title: Driver exits Miramar Hotel illegally, leaves Stomper confused

A car was caught driving recklessly along Havelock Road, making a right turn from a left turn-only exit at Hotel Miramar on May 17 at 7.43pm. Dashcam footage submitted by Stomper Shahberg shows a car attempting to make a right turn from Hotel Miramar’s left turn-only exit towards Saiboo Street. In the video, the car is seen cutting across the traffic junction unexpectedly, prompting Shahberg to quickly brake. The Stomper can be heard honking repeatedly and saying: “Woah, where is he going?” Shahberg told Stomp: "This type of driving should stay away from the road."
