Title: Chairs move and paper gets thrown at man who is alone in Malaysian telco office

The seventh month is upon us. And along with it come a galore of  spooky  happenings and sightings. Take this incident that happened in the office premises of Malaysian telco Tune Talk. CCTV footage that the company uploaded on their Facebook page shows a man who was alone in the office encountering multiple  creepy  happenings. When he was seated at his desk, working on his computer, the chair next to him spun on its own. As the man moved closer to investigate, another chair behind him moved as well, but this goes unnoticed by the him. But he did notice when a stack of paper was flung at him by an unseen force. He immediately stood up and made a phonecall, visibly spooked by what happened.
