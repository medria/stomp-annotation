Title: 2 bikers skid on expressway after trying to avoid car that changed lane

Two bikers skidded on the expressway after a car changed lanes on the expressway. Based on the timestamp on the video posted to the Motorist.sg Facebook page, the incident is said to have taken place on Jan 12 at 8.34am. In the video, a car signals to change lanes on the jam-packed expressway.  The car then attempted to change into the rightmost lane but braked, causing a biker near the vehicle to skid and fall off his bike. Barely a second later, a second biker skidded and fell off his bike too. "O M G not 1, but 2 bikes skidded to avoid a car that chose to change lane without concerning other road users," read the caption on the video.
